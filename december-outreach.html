The campaign

A Christmas gift. A reminder that they matter.

Our December campaign is about preparing Christmas presents for children and sharing the warmth of the festive season. Through thoughtful gifts, we hope to remind each child we reach that they are loved, valued and appreciated.

You can help by contributing towards Christmas presents or speaking with the team about gifts and supplies you would like to donate. Together, these acts of kindness can bring us a step closer to a Christmas where every child feels included.

We will share the confirmed location, date, number of children and fundraising budget as plans are finalized. Please contact us to agree on suitable gifts before purchasing or donating items.

Before contributing, ask our team about the current plans and intended use of your donation.

Before you give

A few things to know.

Who is organizing this campaign?

VOYAG—Voices of Young African Girls—is currently an unregistered initiative. Ask the team to identify the person receiving your contribution on VOYAG’s behalf. We do not promise tax-deductible donations.

Can I pay directly on this website?

Not at present. This page connects you with the team to confirm an appropriate payment or handover arrangement. We do not collect card details here.

How much should I give?

You do not need to decide an amount before contacting us. Ask about the confirmed budget and current needs, then choose what works for you.
