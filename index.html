Who we are

A voice for possibility.

VOYAG stands for Voices of Young African Girls. Our draft mission is to empower girls through education, mentorship, and leadership opportunities, building confidence, emotional intelligence, and values that help them shape their communities.

Education

Learning and practical guidance that expand opportunity.

Mentorship

Supportive relationships that help girls discover their strengths.

Leadership

Space to speak, serve, and contribute with purpose.
