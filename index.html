Who we are

A voice for possibility.

VOYAG stands for Voices of Young African Girls. Our draft mission is to empower girls through education, mentorship, and leadership opportunities, building confidence, emotional intelligence, and values that help them shape their communities.

Our story

From a school club to a growing voice for girls.

At Our Lady of Lourdes in the beautiful town of Bamenda, five young women shared a simple conviction: girls deserved more space to speak, to be heard, and to discover who they could become.

With the guidance of their matron, Sister Mary Kemji, they founded Voices of Young African Girls (VOYAG) on October 19th 2019, 6 years ago. Inspired by their patron saint, Saint Josephine Bakhita, they built a school club where girls could come together and raise their voices. VOYAG’s story was also shared on Bamenda Catholic TV. The club continues at Lourdes today.

As members moved to different countries, VOYAG’s work beyond the school paused. The purpose that brought them together stayed with them. Now, they hope to carry that spirit beyond Lourdes, creating more opportunities for girls to be heard, supported, and encouraged to lead.

On October 19, 2026, VOYAG begins a new chapter—and invites others to help write it.
